## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes traditional Tunisian architectural vernacular and medina artisanal crafts into a contemporary civic technology platform. The aesthetic rejects sterile administrative coldness in favor of a tactile, sun-warmed atelier atmosphere. It serves citizens, municipal leaders, civil society advocates, and local artisans engaging in public discourse, participatory budgeting, civic education, and municipal governance.

The interface pairs the architectural precision of whitewashed stone, arched doorways, and wrought iron nail-heads (*studs*) with digital utility. Surfaces feel physically constructed from limestone and pressed clay rather than rendered on glass. Visual motifs draw directly from Mediterranean door framing, zellige ceramic geometry, and hand-tooled metalwork, translated into clean structural components, generous spacing, and deliberate physical feedback.

## Colors

The palette grounds modern civic interactions within the environmental light and pigment heritage of Tunisia:

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: Base backgrounds use raw warm sand `#F6EFE3`, with interactive cards and elevated panels resting on limestone off-white `#FCF8F2`. Deep structural card backgrounds can use muted linen `#EFE5D5`.
- **Text & Structure**: Primary text uses deep ink brown-black `#2A211B`, replacing clinical digital black with the softness of gallnut calligraphic ink. Secondary copy uses diluted wash `#5E4F43`, and disabled states resolve to `#9C8C7D`.
- **Primary Accent (`#1F4E8C`)**: Inspired by the coastal architecture of Sidi Bou Said, this cobalt-indigo handles primary navigation, active status, authoritative civic badges, and institutional actions.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C8482B`)**: Harissa red-orange acts as the focal accent for primary interactive call-to-actions, voting deadlines, critical alerts, and candidate highlights.
- **Tertiary & Functional Accents**:
  - Olive Green (`#6B7A3A`): Signals validated civic actions, successful submissions, and vocational training statuses.
  - Saffron Ochre (`#D9A441`): Reserves itself for ministerial notices, warnings, pending civic milestones, and highlighting featured bills.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Structural boundaries utilize warm clay sand `#E6DAC8`, while subtle inner borders, stud markers, and active inset lines adopt aged brass sand `#D5C7B2`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances classical Mediterranean literacy with contemporary legibility across dual-script interfaces (Latin and Arabic).

- **Editorial & Section Headings**: Powered by Noto Serif (paired with Amiri or Noto Naskh Arabic for bilingual implementations), bringing the gravitas of historical civic charters and Mediterranean stone-carved inscriptions.
- **Interface & Operational Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans (paired with Cairo for Arabic body copy) provides open counters, balanced x-heights, and clear numerical readouts for municipal data and forms.
- **Rhythm & Line Length**: Editorial passages must maintain a 60–75 character measure to ensure calm readability against warm, textured backgrounds. Avoid ultra-condensed letter tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional architectural structure rooted in courtyards and porticos:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: A 12-column responsive grid with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers center at a maximum width of `1280px`. Asymmetric groupings (e.g., 5-column primary debate thread vs. 7-column civic brief) reflect organic medina alley configurations.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: An 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Collapsible contextual drawers mirror inner riad partitions.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A 4-column layout with `1rem` horizontal margins and `1rem` gutters. Full-width cards nest inside soft clay border rails.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a strict 4px/8px modular cadence. Content groupings rely on generous spatial voids (`space-xl`) rather than harsh dividing rules to preserve a serene, unhurried civic discourse.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile, warm, and sunlit, emulating physical materials cut from sandstone, cedarwood, and hand-cast tiles under high Mediterranean daylight:

- **Surface Layering**: The primary viewport is the foundational stone (`#F6EFE3`). Interactive containers sit elevated as carved blocks of plaster (`#FCF8F2`). Sunken fields (inputs, code blocks, inactive areas) drop to `#EFE5D5`.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadows**: Shadows do not use harsh charcoal; they take on an amber-tinted terracotta base (`rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.08)` to `rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.14)`).
  - *Tier 0 (Resting Cards)*: `0 1px 3px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.03)` with a solid `1px` border of `#E6DAC8`.
  - *Tier 1 (Interactive / Hovered)*: `0 4px 12px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.07), 0 2px 4px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.04)`.
  - *Tier 2 (Modals / Overlays)*: `0 12px 32px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.06)`.
- **Wrought Details & Ceramic Motifs**: Subtle ornamental header panels can incorporate a 1px geometric zellige-tile hairline border at their bottom edge, rendered in 15% opacity Sidi Bou Said Blue. Floating action points and key section corners are accented with tiny 4px circular brass-stud indicators (`#D5C7B2`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by clean rectilinear utility softened with Mediterranean portal geometry:

- **The Arch Motif**: Hero display cards, main dialogue modals, and primary tabs adopt a modified top radius: top corners are shaped with prominent curves (`1.5rem` or `2rem`), while bottom corners remain softly grounded (`0.5rem`), evoking the iconic horseshoe arch doorways of the medina.
- **Standard UI Elements**: Buttons, inputs, and functional badges use a uniform `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius for sturdy hand-crafted presence.
- **Stud Accents**: Micro-elements—such as timeline nodes, checkbox unselected states, and corner fasteners—take perfect geometric circular forms that mimic decorative iron studs on traditional heavy timber doors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA / Harissa)**: Background `#C8482B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover deepens to `#A93B22` with a `0 2px 8px rgba(200, 72, 43, 0.25)` tactile glow. Radius `0.5rem`.
- **Civic Primary (Sidi Bou Said Blue)**: Background `#1F4E8C`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#183E70`. Used for institutional submissions and municipal approvals.
- **Secondary / Craft Outline**: Background transparent, text `#2A211B`, border `1.5px solid #D5C7B2`. On hover, background shifts to `#FCF8F2` with border `#1F4E8C`.
- **Affordance**: Active state shifts down 1px with reduced shadow, confirming physical contact.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Surface `#FCF8F2`, border `1px solid #E6DAC8`, text `#2A211B`. Focus state transitions border to `#1F4E8C` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(31, 78, 140, 0.15)`. Radius `0.5rem`. Labels are set in `label-md` with `space-xs` bottom clearance.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Checkboxes use an off-white box bounded by `#D5C7B2` (`1.5px`). When selected, they fill with `#1F4E8C` and show an ivory-white check mark. Radios feature a center brass dot reminiscent of an architectural nailhead.

### Cards & Porticos
- **Standard Card**: Layered surface in `#FCF8F2` with a `1px solid #E6DAC8` border. Padding `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- **Portal / Civic Card (Arch Top)**: Upper corners set to `1.5rem`, bottom corners to `0.5rem`. Header areas may feature a delicate geometric tile lattice separator line (`border-bottom: 1px dashed #D5C7B2`).
- **Petition & Candidate Card**: Includes an offset status ribbon in Olive Green (`#6B7A3A`) or Harissa Red-Orange (`#C8482B`) in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Chips & Badges
- **Informational Badges**: Background `rgba(31, 78, 140, 0.08)`, text `#1F4E8C`, radius `0.25rem`.
- **Ministerial / Ochre Badges**: Background `rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.15)`, text `#8F6410`, radius `0.25rem`.
- **Filter Chips**: Pill shape (`9999px`), background `#EFE5D5`, text `#5E4F43`. Active filter changes to `#1F4E8C` text on `#FCF8F2` with a `1.5px` border.

### Civic Deliberation & Timeline Nodes
- **Town Hall Discussion Feed**: Nested replies alternate subtly between `#FCF8F2` and `#F6EFE3`, connected by a vertical `2px` track in `#E6DAC8`.
- **Milestone Steppers**: In-progress steps show an Olive Green `#6B7A3A` fill with a center brass-colored dot; uncompleted steps remain hollow circular studs in `#D5C7B2`.
